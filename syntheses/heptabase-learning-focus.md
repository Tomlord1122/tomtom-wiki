## How Tom wants to learn

The Life board and a few unplaced cards record a method, not a subject. Prefer a hard primary source over a pile of secondary summaries. Parse the PDF into a form the model must actually read, instead of trusting a retrieval pass to pick "relevant" chunks. Put the pieces on a whiteboard, then split a long note into one idea per card once the pile is big enough. Pick material that is difficult but still reachable with a tutor. English in the notes should stay clear, and the tutor should ask questions rather than finish the thinking.

That method is why the course notes are worth more than the two-line stubs. A stub titled "The Next-Token Machine" is a bookmark, not an understanding yet.
