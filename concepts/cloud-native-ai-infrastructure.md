# Cloud-Native AI Infrastructure

The CNCF annual survey is a useful current signal for how cloud-native infrastructure is adapting to AI workloads. Kubernetes, observability, networking, security, and platform engineering form the operational substrate; AI workloads add pressure around accelerators, scheduling, data movement, cost, and reliability.

**Working implications:** design AI infrastructure as a platform capability rather than a one-off cluster. Keep workload portability and operational feedback visible, while validating vendor and survey claims against the linked primary report.
