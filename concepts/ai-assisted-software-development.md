# AI-Assisted Software Development

DORA's 2025 research characterizes AI as an amplifier: it magnifies the strengths and weaknesses of the surrounding organizational and technical system. Tool adoption alone is therefore a weak success strategy; delivery practices, platform quality, feedback loops, and team conditions determine whether the investment returns value.

**Working implications:** measure AI-assisted development alongside delivery and reliability outcomes, improve the system around the tool, and treat generated code as an input requiring review, tests, and secure delivery practices.
