--- title: Announcing the AI Gateway Working Group source_url: https://www.kubernetes.dev/blog/2026/03/09/announcing-ai-gateway-wg/ captured_at: 2026-07-16 --- [Captured source: Kubernetes Contributors Blog] The Kubernetes community announced an AI Gateway Working Group focused on standards and best practices for networking infrastructure that supports AI workloads in Kubernetes environments. In the Kubernetes context, an AI Gateway refers to network gateway infrastructure, including proxy servers and load-balancers, that generally implements the Gateway API specification with enhanced capabilities for AI workloads. It is not defined as a distinct product category. The infrastructure is intended to enforce policy on AI traffic, including token-based rate limiting for AI APIs, fine-grained access controls for inference APIs, payload inspection for intelligent routing, caching, and guardrails, and support for AI-specific protocols and routing patterns. The working group's goals include developing declarative APIs, standards, and guidance for AI workload networking, and collaborating with Kubernetes SIGs and sub-projects.
